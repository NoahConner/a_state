Car insurance in Houston is often more expensive than the Texas average due to heavy traffic, high accident rates, uninsured drivers, and weather-related risks such as flooding and hailstorms. Recent market data show that Houston drivers pay around $270 per month for full-coverage auto insurance, while minimum coverage can range from roughly $157 monthly, depending on driving history, ZIP code, vehicle type, and coverage level.
